About Sasi

Sasi Scott is a gigging musician and multi-instrumentalist encompassing guitar, bass, vocals, and trumpet. Currently a Recording Arts major at Butler Tech School of the Arts, Sasi has extensive experience performing as a solo artist and with a band at venues like Music Collective Hamilton, Lori's Roadhouse, and Madison Live!

Bringing live music to the stage.

Education & Honors

  • Butler Tech School of the Arts - Recording Arts Major (2024-)
  • Miami University Middletown - 30+ CCP college credits earned, Dean's List
  • National Honors Society & National Ed Equity Lab Honors Society

Skills & Leadership

  • School of the Arts Student Ambassador (2024-2025)

  • Social media marketing, Networking, & Team Leadership
